Rail Cover Customizer

Upload one or more images, then drag to move, use the corner handles to resize, and the top handle (or shift + scroll, or the [ ] keys) to rotate. The image shows normally on the flat face and wraps at a 45° angle down the beveled edges. Reorder layers by dragging them in the list below.

    Download both the JPG and PNG for each rail cover, then email them to ross@regulusarms.com with your GunBroker order number as the subject line. You can send them as a zip file or as individual image attachments.

    Drag inside the outline to move it, drag a corner to resize. Rotate by dragging the blue dot above it, holding shift while scrolling over the image, or pressing the [ and ] keys.

    Scroll to zoom the workspace (100%), hold the middle mouse button to pan — this only changes your view, not the image size. Double-click the workspace to reset.
